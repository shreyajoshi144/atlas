Operator note. This page describes how the Atlas software behaves. It is not legal advice and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. The organisation running this deployment should review and replace it with its own terms before inviting users.

What Atlas stores

  • Account: your name, email address and a password hash (Argon2id).
  • Your work: research questions, generated cases and notes, Playground conversations, benchmark runs, workflows and run history, and routing policies.
  • Usage records: per-call model usage (calls, tokens, fallbacks and estimated cost) so you can audit what ran.

Who can see it

Your cases, conversations, workflows, benchmark runs and routing policies are tied to your account and are not listed for other accounts. The operator of the deployment, and anyone with access to its database, can technically access stored data.

Third-party services

To do its work Atlas sends your questions and retrieved text to the model and search providers the operator has configured, and fetches pages from the public web. Their handling of that data is governed by their own terms. This website also loads fonts from Google Fonts.

Sessions

Signing in stores a session token in your browser (in local storage if you choose “Keep me signed in”, otherwise for the tab only). Signing out removes it. Tokens expire; seven days by default.

Deletion

You can delete individual cases, conversations and workflows in the app. For account deletion or an export of your data, contact the operator.
