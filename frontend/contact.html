Atlas deployment

Atlas is run by the organisation that deployed it. Questions about your account, data or these terms go to them.

Common requests

  • Forgotten password. Use password reset. If no email arrives, ask the operator; email delivery must be set up for this deployment.
  • Deleting your data. Delete cases, conversations and workflows in the app, or ask the operator to remove the account.
  • Reporting a security problem. Tell the operator directly and do not publish details first.
